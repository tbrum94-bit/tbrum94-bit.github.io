Focal project

$205.95M projected Year-1 revenue

Peloton × Zwift growth strategy — MBA team of 6, University of Georgia

Problem
Peloton was bleeding members — Connected Fitness subscriptions fell 176,000 in a year while the company spent $300M+ on marketing for zero net subscriber growth. A retention and growth crisis.
What we did
Built a member-economics model (LTV of $1,975 at 68% margin and 1.5% monthly churn), analyzed the full competitive set, and designed a Zwift partnership-to-acquisition path into the high-value endurance-athlete segment — projecting $205.95M in Year-1 revenue ($176.4M recurring subscriptions + $29.55M hardware). Every number was our own.
My role
Owned the Zwift acquisition thesis and the multi-year plan behind it — the partner-to-integrate-to-acquire path into the endurance-athlete market that anchors the $205.95M Year-1 projection.
